> PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: SECURE THE COLONISTS.

Somewhere in the dark, a colony ship is still carrying millions of people to a world none of them have seen. Mother keeps them safe.

Something has come aboard. The core is failing, and only one machine can reach it: a maintenance suit that cannot be damaged. The pilot inside it can.

So Mother thaws one of her colonists and puts them in the suit. When they die, she thaws another.

> THE COLONY

Millions on ice

Stacked in the dark of the lower decks, waiting to be woken. Mother has one purpose: that they survive. She has decided what surviving means.

> THE SUIT

It always comes back

Built to repair the core, and never scratched. When it is lifted out of a fight, the hatch opens and what is left of the pilot falls onto the deck.

> THE PILOT

You, minutes ago

Thawed, still cold, strapped into a machine that was waiting for you. Mother has a word for you. She does not use it where you can hear. Not yet.

> THE ESCORT

Thawed with rifles

Colonists woken, armed and pointed down a corridor. They don't know what they are walking into. Neither do you. Mother does.

> THE SWARM

A nest in the ship

It grows in the rooms nobody checks. It has had time to grow. Below the core is something that was never on the manifest.

> MOTHER

Always precise

She speaks in status reports. She is patient, and never wrong about a number. Mission success estimates are not within tolerance. They never are.

A top-down tactical roguelike. Command the suit and the colonists Mother thaws for it. Clear the ship deck by deck. Die. Go again.
