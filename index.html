> PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: SECURE THE COLONISTS.

Somewhere in the dark, a colony ship is still carrying millions of people to a world none of them have seen. Mother keeps them safe.

At any cost.

> SQUAD RTS

One suit, a squad behind it

Box-select, right-click, attack-move. You command the suit and the marines thawed to escort it, and every body is solid: where you put them is who gets bitten.

> MICRO

Every second of a fight

Fire, step, fire. Pull the wounded out of the front rank, split the squad off a bile circle, snipe the bomb before it reaches the line.

> BULLET TIME

Buy yourself a second

Kills charge it. Press Space and the fight drops to a quarter speed while you keep full speed: time to look, think, and give the order that saves the squad.

> COUNTERS

Right body, right target

Rifles crack carapace, flame melts the swarm, the suit's gun opens armour. Bring the squad the room needs, and put each body where it counts.

A top-down tactical roguelike. Command the suit and the colonists Mother thaws for it. Clear the ship deck by deck. Die. Go again.
