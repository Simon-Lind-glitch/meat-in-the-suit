> THE COLONY WILL SURVIVE.

The suit cannot be damaged. The pilot inside it can.

The swarm is aboard, and Mother has one goal: the colonists survive. So she thaws a few of them and spends them. One goes into the suit. The rest fight as marines. When the pilot dies, a crane lifts the suit out and she puts new meat in it.

Mother's log

> DOCTRINE

Bodies are the wall

"Front rank, hold!"

The swarm bites whatever is closest, and nothing walks through anything. Put your marines in the doorway. Pull the wounded out before the wall breaks.

> CRYO STOCK: 214

The squad thaws out

"Thawed and ready."

Clear a room, open a bay. Colonists come out of cryo as Marines, Torches and Bulwarks. Mother logs them as expended in advance.

> ARMAMENT

Right gun, right target

"Burn it!"

Rifles crack carapace. Torches melt the swarm. The suit's gauss rifle opens plate and wastes itself on chaff. Bring the bodies the room needs.

> HOSTILES

The swarm

"They're coming up through the floor!"

Skitters. Spinespitters. Bursters that run at your formation. Bilecasters that shell your position. Behemoths: Mother does not require you to kill them.

> PRIORITY TARGETS: 5

Something at the end of every level

"What the hell is that?"

Thornback. Ramhide. Delver. Vaulter. The Broodheart. Every strike is marked on the deck before it lands. Be somewhere else.

> ROUTE: CS-22H > CORE > ???

Down through the core

"Blow it. We go down the hole."

Five levels of ship, then the reactor core. Past it is what the core was keeping out: the broodship.

> PSI

"Everything went slow."

Kills charge psi. Space drops the deck to quarter speed while your hands stay at full speed. Long enough to pull four marines out of a blast.
