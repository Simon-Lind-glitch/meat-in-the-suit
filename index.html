A top-down tactical roguelike

Meat in the Suit

The suit always comes back.
The pilot inside it does not.

You control one heavy suit and the squad behind it: click to move, attack-move, kite, peek corners, time your abilities. No base, no economy. A corridor, something at the end of it, and the question of whether you can afford to walk in.

When the pilot dies, the suit is recovered. A crane lifts it, the hatch opens, and what is left of the pilot falls out onto the deck. A new body is loaded in. The suit remembers. You don't.

Positioning is the aggro system

The swarm bites whatever is closest, and every body is solid. A squad in a corridor mouth is a wall. It's also the thing being eaten, so it's a wall on a timer.

Your army thaws out of cryo

Marines, Torches and Bulwarks come out of cryo bays you earn by clearing rooms. They aren't reinforcements. They're currency with legs.

Counters, not bigger numbers

Slugs shatter carapace, fire melts the swarm, spines open plate. The suit is built to kill armour and is bad at chaff, so the squad has to handle the swarm.

A boss at the end of every level

Thornback, Ramhide, Delver, Vaulter and the Broodheart. Every attack is painted on the floor first, so the question is always where your bodies are standing.

The ship, and what is under it

Clear the ship level by level down to the reactor core. Blow it, and the next run goes through the hole into the broodship, which is not made of steel.

Buy time, not stats

Kills charge psi. Press Space and the sector slows to a quarter speed while your hands stay at full speed: long enough to pull four marines out of a blast circle.

The workshop between runs

Fit modules to the suit, earn new guns by losing the troopers that carry them, and pick which pilot goes in next. Each one has a quirk and a cost.

Everything is made of boxes

Units, the suit, the aliens, the explosions. A dark, low-fi installation lit room by room, with a dithered picture that looks like 1997 on purpose.

A bestiary that asks questions

Skitters, spinespitters, bursters that run at your formation, bilecasters that shell your position, and behemoths: the one fight you're allowed to walk away from.
